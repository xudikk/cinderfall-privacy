# Cinderfall: Realm of Ashes: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

This policy explains what the mobile game **Cinderfall: Realm of Ashes** ("the game"), published
by **Xudoyberdi Egamberdiyev** ("we"), does with information on your device.

## Short version
- We do not collect, store or sell personal data. The game has no accounts, no analytics,
  no advertising, no in-app purchases and no tracking.
- Your progress and settings stay on your device.

## Information stored on your device
The game saves your settings (language, graphics, sound, controls) and campaign progress in the
app's private storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you
uninstall the game.

## Local network play
The game asks for network access only for the optional **local multiplayer** mode (two phones
on the same Wi-Fi). When you host or look for a local game, the game sends small messages on
your local network that contain the game version, the chosen map and your device's name, so
the other player can find you. These messages stay inside your local network; they are not sent
to us or to any server.

## Children
The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
