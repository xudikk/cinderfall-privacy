# Cinderfall: Realm of Ashes: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 November 2026_

This policy explains what the mobile game **Cinderfall: Realm of Ashes** ("the game"), published
by **Xudoyberdi Egamberdiyev** ("we"), does with information on your device.

## Short version
- The game has no accounts. We do not sell personal data and we do not run our own analytics.
- Your progress and settings stay on your device.
- The free version shows ads through **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device identifiers and
  similar data, as described below. Buying the full game removes interstitial and banner ads.
- The full game is sold as a one-time in-app purchase through **Google Play**. We never see your
  payment details.

## Information stored on your device
The game saves your settings (language, graphics, sound, controls), campaign progress and whether
you own the full game in the app's private storage on your phone. This data is not sent to us and
is deleted when you uninstall the game.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads (between missions, a banner in menus and on the pause and result
screens, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for an in-game bonus). Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service
of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and share
with Google and its ad partners:
- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
- approximate location derived from your IP address;
- app interactions (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped);
- crash logs and diagnostics.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your
consent before ads are personalized, and you can change that choice at any time under
**Settings → Ad privacy**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android
settings. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## In-app purchase (Google Play)
The full game is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we only receive
confirmation that the purchase is valid, so the game can unlock it on your device. Google's
Privacy Policy applies to the payment.

## Online multiplayer
If you play online, the game connects to our relay server (hosted on Cloudflare) over an encrypted
connection. The relay only passes game moves between the players in a match. It does not ask for
your name or any personal information and we do not store match data. Like any internet service,
the hosting provider processes your IP address to deliver the connection.

## Local network play
For local multiplayer (two phones on the same Wi-Fi), the game sends small messages on your local
network that contain the game version, the chosen map and your device's name, so the other player
can find you. These messages stay inside your local network.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from them.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: **xudikk.1@gmail.com**
